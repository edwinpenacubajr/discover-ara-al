ABOUT ARA-AL

Barangay Ara-al

Ara-al is one of the barangays of La Carlota City, Negros Occidental, situated near the foothills of Mount Kanlaon.

Covering more than 1,900 hectares, the community is known for its agricultural lands, mountain scenery, waterfalls, and Sitio Guintubdan.

About Ara-al

A community among the mountains

Barangay Ara-al is one of the communities of La Carlota City, Negros Occidental, situated toward the foothills of Mount Kanlaon.

The area is known for its mountain environment, agricultural communities, waterfalls and nature destinations.

One of its most recognized areas is Sitio Guintubdan, a popular mountain destination and jump-off area for visitors exploring nearby natural attractions.

Our Story

Living in the Shadow of Kanlaon

Barangay Ara-al and the mountain community of Guintubdan have experienced eruptions, ashfall, evacuations and periods of volcanic unrest from Mount Kanlaon.

COMMUNITY & RESILIENCE

Ara-al Continues Forward

Beyond the images of ashfall and eruptions is a community that continues to live, work, farm, and move forward. The story of Ara-al and Guintubdan is also a story of community, cooperation, and respect for the natural environment surrounding Mount Kanlaon.

Despite the challenges of living near an active volcano, the people of Ara-al continue to demonstrate strength, unity, and resilience. Families work to maintain their livelihoods, preserve their traditions, and support one another during difficult times.

For many residents, Mount Kanlaon is more than a natural landmark. It is part of their home, identity, and everyday life. Through changing seasons and uncertain circumstances, the community remains connected to the land and the natural beauty that surrounds it.

As Ara-al continues forward, its story serves as a reminder that resilience is not only about overcoming challenges but also about preserving hope, strengthening community bonds, and building a better future for the next generation.
