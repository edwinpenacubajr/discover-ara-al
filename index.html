About Ara-al

A community among the mountains

Barangay Ara-al is the largest barangay in La Carlota City, Negros Occidental, covering approximately 1,920.59 hectares.

Located toward the foothills and slopes near Mount Kanlaon, Ara-al is known for its cool mountain environment, natural waterfalls, agricultural communities and eco-tourism destinations.

One of its most recognized areas is Sitio Guintubdan, a popular mountain destination and a major jump-off area for visitors exploring the surrounding natural attractions.

Culture & Community

Festivals & Events

Experience the culture, traditions, and community celebrations of Ara-al and La Carlota City.

COMMUNITY

Local Barangay Celebrations

The community of Barangay Ara-al and Sitio Guintubdan celebrates local traditions influenced by highland culture, agriculture, faith, and the community's close connection to the surrounding mountains and nature.

ANNUAL FESTIVAL

Pasalamat Festival

One of La Carlota City's major celebrations, the Pasalamat Festival is a thanksgiving celebration featuring colorful street dancing, cultural performances, vibrant costumes, and festivities celebrating the city's agricultural heritage.
